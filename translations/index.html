    ♡ Terms of Service & Preferred Credits ♡

    First of all, whenever you credit me, please use "thekawaiilotus" or "thekawaiilotus on GitHub". Thank you!

    You can:

    • Take and post screenshots of my translations, so long as they are not the entire story. I would appreciate if you credit me, but it is not mandatory.
    • Share links to my translations on masterlists or external websites. While I'm not opposed to this at all, I ask that you give me a heads-up first (preferably via email).
    • Use my translations for art/comic strips. Crediting is optional!
    • Include my translations in theory/character analysis videos (or other videos of that nature). Please credit me in the description/video somewhere, and before you release the video I just would like a little heads-up (preferably via email).
    • Send me requests! While I do take translation requests, it is up to me if I choose to do them or not (depends on how many other translations I'm doing and if someone has already translated what has been requested). Usually requests find themselves at the end of my backlog unless they're something short (like a song or something), so I may not get to them immediately.

    DON'T:

    • Attempt to profit off of my translations, which are entirely nonprofit to begin with. Any attempt to profit off of them is entirely illegal!
    • Make videos of my translations over recordings of the story. I would just not prefer this at all, since it's against Happy Elements' wishes.
    • Feed my translations into any AI software, bots (Character AI), or things of that nature.

    ♡ How to Contact Me ♡

    If you ever find some sort of mistake in my translations, a coding/formatting error (of which there will probably be plenty lol), or simply just want to ask me a question, then you can reach me any of the following ways:

    • Email: thekawaiilotus.translates@gmail.com
    • Tetratto (Ask Box)
    • Tumblr

    (Note: I will likely check email and Tetratto more frequently)

Translation Notes

[1] – The language Keito uses is more similar to “without losing spirit, let’s take on this final challenge”, which I think fits the very imperial-like Japan themes that Akatsuki has as a unit, but it doesn’t really make much sense in terms of a performance standpoint, especially when translated over. So, I decided to alter the translation slightly, as the word he uses for “spirit” (気) can also mean “heart” and “taking on this final challenge” is very similar to the idea of putting all your effort into something.

[2] – I have decided to bold any word that Tsukasa says in English, that way it’s easy to distinguish.

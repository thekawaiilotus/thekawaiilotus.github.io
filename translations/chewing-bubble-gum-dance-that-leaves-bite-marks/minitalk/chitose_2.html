First Talk

Ughhh, I’m really sorry……I already said I’m sorry, and I don’t know what else to say, but I’m sorry.

How am I this useless……? Producer-san is probably already annoyed having to deal with an idol like me……

Did you enjoy the meal with everyone? To think that I made a mistake and went to the wrong place……

I was worried about you.

Yeah, I know. In the end, my phone was found dead in the corner of my room.

Afterwards, Nozomi-san said I should’ve used a pay phone or asked someone else for help, but I didn’t think of that.

Instead, I just waited until everyone finished lunch. I’m sorry……

Let’s have a second go then.

Huh, this time Producer-san is inviting me?

And you’ll go with me to the place next time? Are you really alright with this?

Ehehe. I can’t fit it into my schedule right now, but I’ll be happy if we can do it sometime……♪

Second Talk

Anyhow. It looks like you were able to deepen your friendship with the other members.

Mashu-san and Juis-san are the type of kids that don’t have any anxiety when talking to strangers, so they get along well with just about anyone. Nozomi-san on the other hand…… might be a little difficult to handle.

It’ll be alright. You’re bound to become close friends with all of them before long, Producer-san……♪

I’ll keep at it.

Fufu, all the kids at ES really love you, Producer-san.

Around Rythlin too, I frequently run into people who talk about you.

……The details? I’d likely get in trouble if I told you what they said, so that’s gotta stay a secret.

Speaking of that……

You were surprised to find out you and Juis-san have something in common? Ah, now that you mention it, Juis-san might’ve also mentioned that.

You were saying it was just a memory from when you were a kid. But, you sounded like you were having fun talking about it……♪

I wish there was some sort of event or something that the two of us could have in common to relate with each other but…… I just have no idea what that would be.

Third Talk

Speaking of, I ended up eating alone at Cafe Cinnamon that day you all went out, and……

The meal was surprisingly delicious. And on top of that, Shiina-san from Crazy:B was working there.

If I asked, I wonder if they would also let me work at Cinnamon……?

You’re not having any difficulties with money, are you?

Huh. No, nothing like that.

Ah, well sometimes it seems like I’m only able to make mistakes, I guess? So, I wanted to put all my heart into something that I could be proud of……

If you work a part-time job, you can still do all sorts of other jobs too, right? Well, now might be too busy to add a new job into the mix.

That’s not possible.

Sure enough, it’s something that’s too unreasonable for me…… I’m so sorry for even thinking about something like this.

……It’s because you want me to focus all my attention on our debut in Japan right now?

—Yeah, that makes sense.  For the sake of the Dears, I need to focus on that before pursuing anything else.

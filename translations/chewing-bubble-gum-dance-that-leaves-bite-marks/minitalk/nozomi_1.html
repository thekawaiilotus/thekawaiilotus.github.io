Bubble Gum Dance || Nozomi Mini-Talk: Day-Off Nuisance

First Talk

—Two coffees. And make one of them the largest size please.

(Juis said he was thirsty, so I decided to come downstairs to the cafe. But it’s far too crowded here.)

(I’ll just get the drinks quickly and then go back to my room.)

H-hello.

…

Why is a peasant like you here?

…Please pay for your items yourself. There is no reason why I should do so.

Is it time for your tea break?

…Peasant. Today is my day off – a day where you have no involvement in my activities – so I think there’s no reason at all for me to share any of my plans with you.

Coffee tastes much better with a small cake on the side, and you think I should buy some…?

I have no need for any of your talk. Please refrain from giving me directions.

Second Talk

—Um. Why did you pay for the entire bill?

The cheapskate Newbie [1] might appreciate something like this, but a gesture of this sort makes me feel nothing.

Surely you don’t plan on taking all the credit for buying Juis a drink…?

You’re mistaken.

…I still don’t believe you, even after you denied it.

First of all, do you always pay for all the dining expenses of the idols you happen to come across by chance?

Today, I’ll accept your actions as a tribute to me… but from now on, gestures like this are useless.

That wasn’t my intention at all.

Is it free of charge because of that coupon you’re holding?

Are you serious? The Newbie should receive items like that. He’s always struggling because of a lack of money anyway.

Even so, just because you produce MELLOW DEAR US doesn’t mean I have any intention to be won over as easily as other ES idols.

Third Talk

Well. The drinks have been prepared, so I will return to my room.

Pardon? Even if we are going back the same way, I did not invite you to come along with me.

Don’t you have somewhere to kill some time before you head back?

To tell you the truth……

Chitose invited you to come have tea with the rest of MELLOW DEAR US…?

I didn’t hear anything about this. Did he purposely try to stop this invite from reaching my ears?

No, Chitose isn’t smart enough to do something like that. There’s no point in making more of a commotion over this, let’s just be on our way.

I need to stop by another store before I leave.

Yes, that sounds good…♪

—Wait a second please. Did you just say something about having tea later?

The other members have already gathered? What are they even thinking?

     

Translation Notes

[1] – Nozomi refers to Mashu as “Newbie” (「新入り」 in Japanese).

First Talk

Well. What does everyone think our outfit change means?

Next, Mashu and the others will be performing our latest song ★

Yaaaaay ♪

Judging by the awe on your faces, you have been waiting impatiently for this moment.

Savor this song to your heart’s content.

This song is more upbeat, which should get everyone excited.

You’re welcome to cheer and clap along to the beat ♪ Everyone, please enjoy!

Alright, Dears!

This is “BUBBLE GUM DANCE”!

Second Talk

Thank you, everyone! That was the best time ever!

The energy in the room just kept rising like crazy! That was sooo much fun!

But, this live show will be ending soon.

Huh, nooo! The time went by way too fast!

Dears, did you enjoy today’s live?

Haha, we had a great time too.

I’m so grateful that our Japan Debut could be celebrated this wonderfully.

Today we received so much support and heard so many cheers, we will treasure those in our hearts…

After today, we will continue to give you our best on stage. Promise!

Third Talk

Mashu and the others will end this by sending all our Dears off!

As you all travel home, savor the blessing of being seen off by yours truly.

Allow today’s live to become your daily bread [1] and ensure you stay in good health.

Fufu. Nozomi said something kind ♪

Well then, see you all later. Please take care on your way home ♪

Translation Notes

[1] – 「日々の糧」 means quite literally “one’s daily bread” which is crazy to me that he uses this specific word, hence why I left it translated as such. He’s basically saying for everyone to have this live be their sustenance.

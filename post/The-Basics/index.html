♡ Quick Lil' Intro ♡

Heyas~ I'm Lotus, and I'm just a girl who's a yapper at heart. I've got a love for languages and anything that involves my creative side (writing, drawing, and other stuff like that). I started my Enstars translation journey over on Tumblr as a way to help me learn Japanese and pick up the language a bit quicker, and then decided my blog was getting a little cluttered, so I made the move over to this website!

I've been in the Enstars community since 2023 and primarily played on the EN Server, but since a little before I started translating, I have switched over to the JP Server as my primary. Anyway, here's some of my favorites in the game:

  • Characters: Nazuna, Kohaku, Tsukasa, Raika, Mashu, and Akiomi
  • Units: Ra*bits and Double Face (mainly, although I've taken a liking to Esupuri, Knights, MDU, and J&A because of the characters I like)

Good news~ I don't particularly have a bias in terms of what stories I translate! Usually it's just whatever happens to catch my eye or if my friends recommend anything.

This last part is quite unrelated to the rest of this translation blog, but I do enjoy other games besides Enstars. Right now, I also play Twisted Wonderland and 18Trip, but I'm unlikely to translate any content for them because they have a really strong translating community currently.
